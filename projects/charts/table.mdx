---

Use `/table` to display aggregated data as a chart formatted as a table.

## How to use

1. Type `/table` and press **Space**.
2. Describe the table you want — for example, *"deals by rep with total amount and count"*.
3. Grapple creates the table chart and navigates you to it.

Or pick **Create from View** to build from an existing view.

## Tips

- Table charts are useful when you need precise values alongside a visual report.
- Wait for materialization to reach **up to date** before the chart loads data.
