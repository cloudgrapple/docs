---

Use `/share` to invite someone to view a project as a workspace guest.

## How to use

1. Type `/share` and press **Space**.
2. Enter one or more email addresses, separated by spaces.
3. Grapple sends an invite email with a link to the project.

Recipients who aren't workspace members get **guest** access. Guests see only projects shared with them — not the full Data Library.

You can also share from **Settings → Share** in the project editor.

## Revoking access

Remove a guest's access by revoking their share on the project or removing them from the workspace.
