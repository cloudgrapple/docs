Grapple bills on **records processed**. One credit is 1 million records.

Each month, divide records processed by 1,000,000 and round to the nearest credit. A half credit rounds up, so 1.5 million records is 2 credits. The year's total adds those credits. Leftover records do not carry into the next month.

Records processed are source records plus project records.

## Sources

Source records are the unique records stored at the end of the month, added across your collections. Syncing the same record again does not count it again. A record removed before month end is not counted. A contact and a deal are both records.

## Projects

Each project counts at most once per day, even if it materializes more than once that day. Add those daily record counts across the month. A day with no materialization adds nothing.

Automations are a separate plan limit, not part of this count. Allowances for every plan are on [Plans](https://estimate.askgrapple.com/plans).

## What does not count

- **Syncing more often.** A record synced ten times in a month is still one source record.
- **Updating a project many times in a day.** That project still counts once that day.
- **Filtering a project.** `/filter`, a grid filter, and a chart filter run after materialization. They do not add records, and they do not shrink that day's count.
- **Adding charts.** A chart reads the materialized project. It does not process records of its own.
- **Asking an AI chatbot,** including Claude.

## What counts

- **More data in a source.** Adding 1,000 records in Salesforce adds 1,000 source records at month end.
- **A new project.** Its materialized records are added once per day, on top of the source records. The source rows are not counted a second time just because a project uses them.

## Use fewer records

<Tip>
  Open a column menu and choose **Filter**. That field filter runs before materialization and drops rows, so fewer records are processed. `/filter` on the project does not change the count.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  On a source, **Start date** defaults to all historical data. Set a later start date to skip that backfill. Older records never sync, so they never count.
</Tip>

## How you pay

Included credits are **prepaid**. The plan fee pays for the year's credits up front.

On an annual plan, those credits are one pool for the year. Monthly credits roll over inside the year, and you can use them in any month of that term. A quiet month keeps its credit. A heavy month can spend credits from other months in the same year.

**Overages are charged monthly in arrears**, and only after the year's credits are used up. Once the running total passes the included credits, each extra credit is invoiced that month at the plan's price per credit. A later quiet month does not reverse that invoice.

Prices and included credits for every plan are on [Plans](https://estimate.askgrapple.com/plans).

## Starter

Starter is $6,000 per year ($500 per month) and includes 12 credits. Each credit past those 12 is $500.

A normal month:

| Line | Records |
| --- | --- |
| Sources at month end | 500,000 |
| Projects, 16,600 records each day for 30 days | 498,000 |
| **Total** | **998,000** |
| **Credits** | **1** |

That month draws 1 of the prepaid 12. There is no overage. The 30-day project line matches the illustration on the plans page. Your month sums the days it actually has.

A heavier month of 1.6 million records rounds to 2 credits. That spends 2 of the 12. There is still no overage.

The first month whose credits push the year past 12 is invoiced in arrears for the credits past 12, at $500 each.
